About Us

Learn how our passion for the ocean inspired a new way of generating sustainable energy.

2024 Founding Year

Born from the Unending Pulse of the Ocean

Founded by a distinguished group of naval engineers and climate scientists in 2024, **Ener-Wave** emerged from a simple yet powerful realization off the Patagonian coast: while solar energy relied on daylight and wind energy depended on unstable gusts, ocean waves never stopped. Their constant motion concealed the greatest untapped energy potential on the planet.

Driven by this discovery and motivated by the global climate urgency, we engineered advanced marine solutions. We developed closed-loop wave energy converters capable of withstanding the ocean's harshest storms, transforming tidal impact into steady, emission-free electricity directly integrated into the onshore grid.

Our Technology

We develop cutting-edge mechanical and software solutions to harness the power of the seas.

Wave Energy

Systems that harness the vertical and horizontal kinetic thrust of surface waves using heavy-duty hydropneumatic oscillating buoys.

  • High operational consistency (94%)
  • Zero acoustic emissions
  • Biodegradable moorings

Tidal Energy

Submerged bidirectional turbine generators that harness incoming and outgoing tidal flows in a highly predictable and secure manner.

  • 100% energy predictability
  • Marine life-friendly design
  • Direct underwater transmission
